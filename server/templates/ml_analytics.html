How to read this page
Priority P1–P4
Where to start. Follows threat likelihood: P1 strongly resembles known attack activity, P4 is unusual but unlike known attacks. The detection's own severity is shown in its evidence.
Threat likelihood
How closely the process resembles known attack activity, from a model trained on real adversary-emulation data. The best single signal for what to look at first.
Rarity
How unusual the process is compared with normal activity on this estate. Rare with a low threat likelihood usually means legitimate but uncommon software (developer tools, vendor agents).
Likely ATT&CK tactic
An advisory mapping, shown only when the model is confident and the lead itself looks malicious. A rule-derived technique always takes precedence.
Why it was flagged
The behaviours that set the process apart. The bars show how far from normal each one is; yes/no indicators show as present.
